SPEAR

A campaign of its own for OpenReliant, on the main menu's GAME MODES: the 205th
Marauders on the day the war comes back. Version 0.1 holds its first mission.

This mod mixes two kinds of material, under two licences.

The mod's own work is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0
(https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/):

  - the manifest mod.ini
  - the scripts menu.luau, records.luau, landing.luau, cinematic.luau and
    wing.luau
  - the mission's source mission301.mission.luau, and mission301.dte, built
    from it with sltool
  - the movies spear_intro.bik and spear_m01.bik, placeholders made for the
    mod: a title card and a test pattern

The radio lines, the *_m1_l*.opus files, were made with Chatterbox (Resemble AI,
MIT licence), cloning voices from StarLancer's own speech: the wingmen's voices
of Face, Juice, Ego, Rhino and Flash, the Reliant's landing control, and the
pilots of a Prowler, a convoy and a Nanny in the campaign's missions. StarLancer
is copyright Digital Anvil, Warthog and Microsoft. The lines' words are the
mod's own.

The faces on the radio, the ships, the music and the rest of what the mission
shows are the game's own, from your copy of StarLancer; Mars is Realistic
Planets', which the mod requires. None of them is in this mod.

This is a fan mod for OpenReliant, which needs a copy of StarLancer to play.
