SPEAR

A campaign of its own for OpenReliant, on the main menu's GAME MODES: the 205th
Marauders on the day the war comes back. Version 0.2 holds its first two
missions.

This mod mixes two kinds of material, under two licences.

The mod's own work is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0
(https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/):

  - the manifest mod.ini
  - the scripts menu.luau, records.luau, landing.luau, cinematic.luau,
    wing.luau, wing2.luau, nanny.luau and campaign.luau
  - the missions' sources mission301.mission.luau and mission302.mission.luau,
    and mission301.dte and mission302.dte, built from them with sltool
  - the movies spear_intro.bik and spear_m01.bik, placeholders made for the
    mod: a title card and a test pattern

The radio lines, the *_m1_l*.opus and *_m2_l*.opus files, were made with
Chatterbox (Resemble AI, MIT licence), cloning voices from StarLancer's own
speech: the wingmen's voices of Face, Juice, Ego, Rhino, Flash and Cutter, the
Reliant's landing control, Fort Baxter's comms officer, and the pilots of a
Prowler, a convoy and a Nanny in the campaign's missions. StarLancer is
copyright Digital Anvil, Warthog and Microsoft. The lines' words are the mod's
own.

The faces on the radio, the ships, the music and the rest of what the missions
show are the game's own, from your copy of StarLancer; Mars and Earth are
Realistic Planets', which the mod requires. None of them is in this mod.

This is a fan mod for OpenReliant, which needs a copy of StarLancer to play.
